# Project Roadmap

The implementation of this multi-vendor marketplace is divided into the following phases:

*   **Phase 1 — Foundation:** Initial Next.js setup, structure, testing configuration, and documentation (Current Phase).
*   **Phase 2 — Authentication and Roles:** Supabase Auth integration, custom claims/profiles, and RLS foundations.
*   **Phase 3 — Vendors:** Vendor registration, approval workflows, and store profiles.
*   **Phase 4 — Categories and Catalog:** System taxonomy and category management.
*   **Phase 5 — Products and Variants:** Vendor product creation, variant management, and image uploads.
*   **Phase 6 — Inventory:** Atomic inventory tracking and management functions.
*   **Phase 7 — Cart:** Persistent shopping carts and server-side calculation.
*   **Phase 8 — Atomic Orders:** Checkout flow, inventory reservation, and multi-vendor order splitting.
*   **Phase 9 — Payments:** Edge Functions for payment gateways and secure webhooks.
*   **Phase 10 — Commissions and Vendor Balances:** Automated fee calculation and vendor ledger management.
*   **Phase 11 — Shipping:** Integration with logistics providers and tracking updates.
*   **Phase 12 — Returns and Refunds:** RMA processes and financial reversals.
*   **Phase 13 — Reviews and Wishlist:** Customer engagement features.
*   **Phase 14 — Coupons and Promotions:** Discount logic and validation.
*   **Phase 15 — Notifications:** Email, SMS, and in-app alerts.
*   **Phase 16 — Admin Panel:** Complete back-office operations and reporting.
*   **Phase 17 — Security Testing:** Penetration testing, RLS auditing, and vulnerability scans.
*   **Phase 18 — Performance:** Query optimization, caching strategies, and load testing.
*   **Phase 19 — SEO:** SSR optimization, sitemaps, and meta tags.
*   **Phase 20 — Production Deployment:** Final CI/CD, domain mapping, and launch.
